Price Guide

The goal of this price guide is to control the hype.  An M498 will NOT "make you mix like the pros".

I plan on posting a critique on all things that pop up on ebay and possibly provide a place where people can buy and sell parts. 

 I admit that these modules are awesome, but they are not the Canadian NEVE.  They are Ward-Becks and are unique and should be treated as unique modules.  Sellers  should not piggy back and compared them to another reputable name.  They should stand by themselves for the quality they represent.
