Dave Thomas - Dr. Beck



"I have metal cases made here in Vancouver that hold two M460/M470 14" modules and I build up my own power supply regulator cards including phantom power. I currently rack M460 or M470 modules including the case, power supplies, connectors and reconditioning of the modules for $495 Cnd a pair.



I have modified the EQ's on M460 and M462 modules to widen the "Q" from a minimum 2 down to 1.5 thereby emulating the wider EQ curve of Neve and API of the era.

I believe that the Ward Beck's are referred to as the Canadian Neve's because Roger Beck was approached by the CBC in the early 70's to see if Ward Beck could build a console that would compare to the Neve consoles of the time utilizing the Neve 1081 modules. Roger designed the M460 modules.

The M460 utilizes a custom made Hammond input transformer with the same winding ratio as the St Ives used in the Neves. The preamp is also discrete class "A" just like the Neve except Roger used a class A/B transistor output stage in the preamp garnishing 6db more headroom in the front end than Neve.

I am in the process of racking some M470 modules, M460La modules and building a rack with 2-M461, 2-M462, 2-M464, 2-M467 and 2-M466 modules. I have a couple of extra M460LA modules arriving late next week and these are available for racking...they would be $1000 cnd reconditioned
and racked in our custom 19" cases with power supplies and all connectors.

I can supply edge connectors for the M460/M470 modules for $7.50 each new."
