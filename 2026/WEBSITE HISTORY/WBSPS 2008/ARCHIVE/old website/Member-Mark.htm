Ward Beck Systems 2488 Recording Console

We have upgraded our console to a mid-70's Ward Beck 2488 console with 24 transformer balanced mic/line inputs. The console has eight subs and four master faders. There are four Ward Beck designed M466 compressors onboard which can be used in mono, as stereo pairs, or quad linked. The sub and channel meters are VU and the stereo master meters are all buffered PPM Meters. Two separate Cue mixes can be provided for headphone monitoring during tracking.

This series of WBS consoles have been regularly compared to older Neve's in build quality and sound. We think it's close but a little cleaner than 80 series Neve's, but hey that's why we've got lots of Neve and API pre-amps and EQ's. We've recently reworked the summing amps to use Neve 1272's which has really beefed up the sound of the two bus to say the least.

Deep, rich, wide and kick ass come to mind.

We have completed an overhaul of our board which included upgrades to the summing busses/amps and output amps as well as new capacitors everywhere. The summing busses/amps and output amps were treated to 1% metal film resisters and new Panasonic HFQ/HFS low impedance capacitors. All other modules have been recapped with the Panasonic's as well. This has significantly reduced the noise floor, improved dynamics and air in all signals. The bottom octaves are well defined and solid, the midrange is present and musical and the top octaves are extremely well defined and clear.

This console was originally installed at the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation - CBC Radio in Windsor.
