Welcome to the Ward-Beck Systems Preservation Society. My goal is to keep vintage Ward-Beck equipment running and working by supplying a database of documentation, knowledge, and support.  So far 31 Manuals have been scanned in their entirety, 65 Remain to be scanned.  I hope to add more manuals as I find them and more pictures as you provide them.

WBSPS would like to thank the good people at Ward-Beck for supporting our website.  They are still making quality products and coming up with ingenious solutions for the broadcast market.  Go to http://www.ward-beck.com/ for more information.

September 1, 2003
The M450B and the L1202B manuals have been scanned and Made into PDF.  This box will take a lot more time then I had originally thought.  It has been sorted and cleaned up.   I've discovered modules I never knew existed, and discovered modules I want!!!  While wiring my newly acquired M472 Modules, I came to the realization that they are un-balanced and the M470A send to the module is also unbalanced.  I have (19) M462B modules on my console and am running them unbalanced...stupid in my opinion.  The reason the M472 is so scaled down and compact is that there was a Stereo Version of this module (M472A), the M472 and M472A share the same design and the same board.  Interesting cost effective piece of gear in my opinion.  M472A is my rack mount pick of the week!

August 22, 2003: 
My M472 Modules came from Bob Malloy from Malloy Sound and Video Contractors and put them into my console that was pre-wired for M462 modules and they didn't work.  I looked at the EQ filler plate and realized the pin configuration was almost identical!  The signal was being bypassed before it even went through the module.  So, the pin outs on the M472 Manual are incorrect.  If you have the pin outs please contact me.  Wayne Barron Has joined the club with his "$200 Thrift store console"
