Module STL Viewer

A 3-D model of every module drawn in a mechanical drawing: the front panel at the size its technical data gives, with the holes, cut-outs and knobs its drawing draws, and the card behind it. Turn it with the mouse, change it on the right, and download the STL. Click the panel plan to add a hole there, shift-click a hole to take it away. Drop any .stl on the view to look at it.
