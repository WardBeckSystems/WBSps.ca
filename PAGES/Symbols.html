Symbols

Every module with its pinout read has its symbol synthesized from its own repository's data (RTO.json): the KiCad symbol, and beside it the same symbol with the module's front panel and back panel as a PowerPoint deck and a Visio document, every part an editable shape. The back panel is each connector seen from the rear, every contact with its signal: what to wire to. Bodies are coloured by the module's kind, pins by theirs (input green, output blue, power red, ground grey, control brown). 170 modules.
