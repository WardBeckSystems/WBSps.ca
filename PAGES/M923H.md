# M923H

M923H is not a module: it is sheet H of the [M923](M923.md) schematic (M923 MASTER/SUBMASTER MODULE, drawing 923-02-001, OrCAD, July 11 1996|29). The top sheet `M923.SCH` (M923 SUMMING/MASTER AMPLIFIER) draws the sheets M923A–M923M; their files and the sheet preview are in the [M923 repository](../WBSps.ca/Github.WardBeckSystems/MODULES.RENAISSANCE.M923/).
