## The series

The R2K ("Renaissance R2K Audio Console") is the successor of the [Renaissance](Series-Renaissance.md): a stereo radio console of R200 modules, drawn from 1998 to 2000. Its technical instruction manual has one section per module, then the console diagrams, the guest turret and the Torpey clock (the remote time display by Torpey Controls & Engineering).

The R201 and R221 stereo input modules accept two balanced stereo inputs, A or B, with front-panel trims for left and right; MONO sums left and right. Stereo balanced insert points come before the FADER and CHANNEL ON switch (bypass jumpers on the board); CUE is pre-fader, pre-CHANNEL ON, to the cue speaker in the meter bridge. The signal can be assigned to stereo PROGRAM 1–4 and MONO MIX 1–2, all buses balanced; the R221 adds front-panel send levels to MONO MIX 1 and 2. A stereo DIRECT output follows CHANNEL ON, and every switch has an LED. DIP switches on each input set its mode of external control: four control inputs and four control outputs (CH ON, CH OFF, A SEL, tallies, cue) in eight preset modes.

Followed by the [R2K Cool Grey](Series-R2K-Cool-Grey.md).
