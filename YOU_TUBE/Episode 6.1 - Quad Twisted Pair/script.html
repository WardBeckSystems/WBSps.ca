Quad Twisted Pair

Four balanced pairs in one jacket. QTP was Ward-Beck's brilliant series of cabling and connecting products: RJ45 and DB25 breakout boards, a QTP version of the POD8A, an XLR encoder, a patchbay, and the RTO-144 that puts a classic Ward-Beck module on two RJ45 jacks.

Timeline

  • 2015|48July 2015 inventory: 70 QTP-DB25 boards in bin BB3. POD8A distribution amplifier offered with a QTP rear panel.
  • 2016|49April 2016: QTP-BAC encoder PCB, revision 17.1, drawn MPK.
  • 2018|51AMS8-1 MKIII and MLC8A manuals: the analog output moves to a DB25, Tascam pinout, "compatible with Ward-Beck's QTP series of cabling and connecting products".
  • 2020|53RTO-144: QTP-A and QTP-B RJ45 jacks, four balanced pairs each, with 48 V phantom switching. Dual M460A rack.
