# Episode 6.1: Quad Twisted Pair (2015|48 – 2020|53)

**Runtime target:** 6 min · **Voice-over:** ~230 words · **Slides:** 24

> Four balanced pairs in one jacket. QTP was Ward-Beck's brilliant series of cabling and connecting products: RJ45 and DB25 breakout boards, a QTP version of the POD8A, an XLR encoder, a patchbay, and the RTO-144 that puts a classic Ward-Beck module on two RJ45 jacks.

### 6.1.1 Cold open: four pairs in one jacket

**VO:** Look at standard Cat5 Ethernet cable. It contains four balanced twisted pairs. Why were we dragging four separate bulky XLR cables across a facility when one lightweight, inexpensive Cat5 cable could carry all four channels with zero interference? Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.

**VO:** And one more thing: the RTO-144 breakout board! It allows you to take any classic vintage Ward-Beck console module and connect it instantly via two RJ45 jacks. Vintage analog royalty, brought seamlessly into the modern patchbay.
