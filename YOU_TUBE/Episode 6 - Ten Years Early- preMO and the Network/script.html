Ten Years Early: preMO and the Network

Mic in, multicast out. From the first RAVENNA and AES67 meters to the preMO-A1 network microphone preamp at NAB 2017 and the preMO-221 and 222 in 2021: Ward-Beck put the microphone on the network a decade before the rest of the industry even understood the problem.

Timeline

  • 2013|46AES67 published by the Audio Engineering Society: interoperable audio over IP, built on RAVENNA and its peers.
  • 2014|47First Ward-Beck network products: 32ME-N, 16 AES3 to and 16 from an AES67 network with 32 meters. AMS8-1AN.
  • 2016|49AMS2-N networked monitor: RAVENNA and AES inputs, 1RU, manual dated August 2016.
  • 2017|50NAB 2017: preMO-A1 remote microphone preamp for RAVENNA / AES67 networks, powered over Ethernet. Rack built with an AMS2 and a PoE switch.
  • 2017|50SMPTE publishes ST 2110 for professional media over IP; preMO-A1 later gains NMOS discovery and ST 2110-30 operability.
  • 2018|51MLC8-N: eight-channel level controller and on-ramp onto a RAVENNA / AES67 network.
  • 2021|54preMO-221 and preMO-222: AES67/70 and SMPTE 2110 dual microphone preamps, with a GPIO option. AMS222 networked monitor.
  • 2021|54January 2021: Ward-Beck announces it will cease manufacturing.

Script and slides

6.1 Cold open: mic in, multicast out

VO Look at the way broadcast audio had been done for fifty years: miles of thick, heavy copper snake cables, point-to-point hum, ground loops. It was slow. It was heavy. It was completely outdated. We asked a simple question: what if the microphone plugged directly into the network? Mic in, multicast out. Boom.

VO In 2013, Audio over IP was just emerging. Ward-Beck didn't wait for proprietary walled gardens—they jumped straight onto open standards: RAVENNA and AES67. Gigabit Ethernet routing with microsecond timing.

6.4 preMO-A1: the microphone on the network

VO At NAB 2017, Ward-Beck dropped a bombshell: the preMO-A1. A broadcast-grade remote microphone preamplifier that converted analog mic signals right at the source and streamed AES67 multicast packets directly onto the plant network.

6.5 Inside the preMO-A1

VO Inside the preMO-A1: pristine ultra-low-noise analog front-ends, high-end 24-bit converters, and dual redundant Gigabit Ethernet ports running Power over Ethernet. No wall warts. No noise. Pure digital perfection.

15. preMO-A1 screen: mic to headphones, input mute, 2021|54

6.6 Why the preamp belongs at the microphone

VO Why run 500 feet of fragile, low-level analog microphone cable across a stadium? Amplify and digitize the signal two inches from the capsule, and send digital packets over fiber or Cat5. The physics are undeniable. It's simply better.
