# Episode 6: Ten Years Early: preMO and the Network (2013|46 – 2021|54)

## Script and slides

### 6.1 Cold open: mic in, multicast out

**VO:** Look at the way broadcast audio had been done for fifty years: miles of thick, heavy copper snake cables, point-to-point hum, ground loops. It was slow. It was heavy. It was completely outdated. We asked a simple question: what if the microphone plugged directly into the network? Mic in, multicast out. Boom.

### 6.5 Inside the preMO-A1

**VO:** Inside the preMO-A1: pristine ultra-low-noise analog front-ends, high-end 24-bit converters, and dual redundant Gigabit Ethernet ports running Power over Ethernet. No wall warts. No noise. Pure digital perfection.

- Slide 15: [preMO-A1 screen: mic to headphones, input mute, 2021|54](media/015%20-%20preMO-A1%20screen-%20mic%20to%20headphones%2C%20input%20mute%2C%202021-54.jpg)

### 6.6 Why the preamp belongs at the microphone

**VO:** Why run 500 feet of fragile, low-level analog microphone cable across a stadium? Amplify and digitize the signal two inches from the capsule, and send digital packets over fiber or Cat5. The physics are undeniable. It's simply better.
