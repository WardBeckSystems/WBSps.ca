# Episode 2.2: The Preamp: Building Blocks (1968|1 – 1988|21)

**Runtime target:** 10 min · **Voice-over:** ~536 words · **Slides:** 59

> Mmm, hello sweetheart... let's talk about where all the good vibrations start: the preamplifier. For twenty glorious years through the 70s and 80s, Ward-Beck crafted input channels with the same irresistible recipe: the M124, the versatile M600, the seductive M441, the modular 460 with its tantalizing plug-in boards, the sweeping M462, the luscious M470, the stereo 490, and the sleek M520.
